Privacy policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. What we collect

Account data: name, organization, email, phone, plan. Portfolio data: properties, units, leases, tenants, invoices, receipts, maintenance records, khatian references — the data you enter to run your business. Usage data: pages visited, features used, device type.

2. How we use it

To operate the Platform, compute taxes and compliance checks, process payments via our gateways (bKash, SSLCommerz), provide the AI caretaker, send billing/statutory reminders, and improve the service. We do not sell your data.

3. Multi-tenant isolation

Your portfolio data is isolated at the database level. Owners never see other owners' data; tenants only see their own unit and lease; partners only see their assigned works. Staff roles access data only within their function and are audited.

4. AI processing

When you chat with KR, your query and relevant portfolio context are processed to generate answers and actions. Tool actions are logged and shown to you. We configure model providers (e.g. DeepSeek) with no-training agreements; your data is not used to train public models.

5. Payments

Payment credentials are handled by our payment partners (bKash, SSLCommerz). We never store your card numbers or wallet PINs. Transaction references are recorded for reconciliation.

6. Retention & export

You can export your data (PDF/XLSX) any time from the workspace. On account deletion, data is erased within 90 days except where law requires retention (e.g. financial records).

7. Security

We use TLS encryption in transit, AES-256 at rest, role-based access control, and regular security reviews. No system is perfectly secure; you share information at your own risk, and we ask you to use strong passwords.

8. Your rights

You may access, correct, export or delete your personal data by contacting privacy@krtaker.com. We respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies & local storage

We use local storage for session and preferences. Marketing pages may use analytics cookies; you can decline via the consent banner.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@krtaker.com · contact page
