How dispatch works

  1. Owner or tenant reports an issue; liability resolved automatically.
  2. Landlord-side work becomes a work order matching your trade.
  3. You receive it in My Works — send a quotation.
  4. Owner approves → you execute → upload completion photos.
  5. Service Manager inspects & signs off → payout released (net of fee + TDS).

Partner pricing

💰 10% platform fee per completed work order, TDS applied per Income Tax Act 2023. No listing fees, no monthly charges — you only pay when you get paid.
