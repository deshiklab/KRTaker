How KRTaker works

From first login to a fully autonomous portfolio — here's the journey, step by step, role by role.

1 · Onboarding (Owner)

  1. Register and choose your plan (Starter / Business / Enterprise).
  2. Add your properties — name, type, jurisdiction, holding number, khatian refs (CS/SA/RS/BS). Add units with floor and square footage.
  3. Invite your team — property managers get scoped access to assigned buildings only.
  4. Invite tenants — they receive a portal login to view their lease and pay rent.
The compliance engine validates every asset against khatian records and flags any jurisdiction gaps before leasing starts.

2 · Leasing with legal gates

  1. Create a lease — pick the unit, tenant, dates, rent and advance.
  2. KRTaker instantly checks the law:
    • Term > 12 months → Pending Registration until sub-registry deed uploaded (TPA §107).
    • Residential advance > 1 month rent → flagged (PRCA §10/§23).
    • Commercial corporate tenant → TDS rate auto-applied on invoices.
  3. Tenant accepts digitally; the ledger activates once registration is verified.

3 · The monthly cycle (automated)

1st — Invoices

KR generates rent invoices for every active lease, splitting TDS for corporate tenants.

Ongoing — Payments

Tenants pay via bKash or SSLCommerz; signed receipts issue automatically (PRCA §13).

5th — Reminders

Overdue tenants get polite automated reminders; escalation rules kick in at day 30.

Monthly — Tax engine

Holding tax recalculated on Net Annual Value; TDS ledger updated for the accountant.

4 · Maintenance & partners

  1. Tenant reports an issue ("roof leak") from the portal or chat.
  2. KR resolves liability — structural → landlord; day-to-day → tenant (PRCA defaults).
  3. Landlord-side work is dispatched to a Service Partner with a quotation.
  4. A Service Manager inspects the completed work, runs QC, and approves payout.
  5. If the landlord ignores a structural ticket, tenants can submit contractor invoices for rent deduction.

5 · Compliance & legal

Registration tracker

Every >12-month lease monitored until its deed is verified.

Eviction monitor

3+ months unpaid → compliant §114A notice generated for legal review.

Statutory calendar

Holding tax, TDS PSR, surcharge, income tax deadlines — with reminders.

Legal team docket

Platform legal counsel (or B2B firms) manage cases inside KRTaker.

6 · The platform behind it

Super Admin

Subscriptions, billing, packages, integrations, partner onboarding, finance, HR.

Accountant

MRR, settlements, payouts, TDS & VAT ledger, financial exports.

CRM & Help Desk

Tickets with SLA, subscriber onboarding, CSAT.

Security

Role-based access + row-level isolation — data is siloed per tenant, always.
