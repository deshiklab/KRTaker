Platform features

Nine feature pillars — portfolio, leases, finance, payments, maintenance, compliance, utilities, security and documents — every one built around Bangladeshi property reality.

🏢 Portfolio management

Properties, units, tenants, leases — with khatian records (CS/SA/RS/BS), jurisdiction mapping and mortgage tracking. Rent roll and occupancy at a glance, from any device.

Properties & units

Multi-building portfolios, floor-by-floor units, status tracking (leased/vacant/maintenance).

Tenant directory

NID/BIN, NRB flags, contact history — every tenant in one place.

Khatian & e-Porcha

Land record cross-reference to verify ownership before leasing.

Manager scoping

Give managers access to only their assigned buildings.

📄 Lease & legal engine

Generate compliant tenancy agreements with automatic statutory gates. The platform refuses to let you break the law — and tells you why.

TPA 1882 §107 registration gate

Leases >12 months stay "Pending Registration" until the sub-registry deed is uploaded and verified.

PRCA 1991 advance cap

Residential advances capped at 1 month rent; rent-controller consent required for overrides.

Rent revision guard

Flags hikes inside the 2-year window (PRCA §15/§16) with justification tracking.

Eviction compliance

No lockouts — §114A compliant notices only when grounds (3+ months unpaid) are proven.

🧾 Finance & taxation

Automated invoicing, TDS, holding tax and wealth-surcharge math — accountant-ready exports.

Rent invoicing

Monthly invoices per lease with due/overdue states and automatic reminders.

TDS split

Corporate tenancies: gross − TDS (ITA 2023 §109/§128) = net, with a full withholding ledger.

Holding tax

Gross annual rent → −2-month allowance → −mortgage interest → Net Annual Value → city rate.

Signed receipts

Cryptographic rent receipts per PRCA §13, printable for courts and banks.

💳 Payments & NRB

bKash Tokenized Checkout

Grant → create → execute → query flow, sandbox-tested, live-ready.

SSLCommerz

Cards, internet banking for commercial rents — IPN-validated webhooks.

NRTA/NITA workflows

Non-resident accounts with compliant credit sources.

Remittance dossiers

Auto-generated IT10B, Form TM, QA-22 and lease docs for AD banks.

🔧 Maintenance & service partners

Liability resolver

KR reads the issue and the lease to decide landlord vs tenant responsibility.

Partner dispatch

Route work to vetted B2B partners with quotations and SLAs.

QC & sign-off

Service Managers inspect completed works, rate partners, approve payouts.

Tenant repair deduction

Validated contractor invoices → automatic rent deduction when landlords neglect.

⚖️ Compliance & AI

Statutory calendar

Holding tax, TDS PSR, surcharge and income-tax deadlines, tracked.

AI Caretaker (KR)

Chat over your portfolio + BD law. Generates invoices, resolves liability, answers PRCA/TPA questions.

Role-based access

10 roles with scoped data — tenants see only their unit; partners only their works.

Reports & exports

PDF rent receipts, XLSX ledgers, VAT summaries — accountant friendly.

⚡ Utility & energy management

Bangladeshi buildings run on shared meters, generators and load-shedding calendars. KR turns that chaos into a ledger.

Shared-meter billing

Water, gas & electricity split per unit — meter entries, allocations and utility bills in one flow.

Generator & load-shedding ops

Diesel logs, run-hours, refuelling schedules and backup duty rosters for the building's own plant.

Solar & net-metering

Export/import tracking with utility credit reconciliation for net-metered rooftops.

CAM billing

Common-area maintenance (lift, security, cleaning) allocated fairly per unit — with tenant-facing breakdowns.

🛡️ Tenant experience & security

e-KYC tenant screening

NID checks, references and rental blacklist review before a lease is signed.

Visitor & gate management

Digital gate passes, visitor logs and guard handover notes for the security desk.

Parking & amenity booking

Allocate parking slots, generator points and community hall bookings with a live calendar.

Notice board & community

Building notices, AGM invites, announcements and a resident directory.

🗄️ Documents, assets & insurance

Document vault

Deeds, khatian, e-porcha, utility bills and agreements — encrypted, versioned, expiry-alerted.

Unit inventory

Furniture & appliance registers per unit with condition photos and handover logs.

Fire-safety calendar

Extinguisher & hydrant inspections, BFSCD checks and evacuation-drill records.

Insurance & renewals

Property and fire policies with coverage summaries and renewal reminders.
