❓ Frequently asked questions

How do I start my 14-day free trial?

Go to Register, create your account with your email, verify the OTP we send you, then log in to the dashboard. Your trial starts automatically — no card required.

How do I add my first building?

Log in → Properties → ＋ Add property. Enter the name, type, city corporation and holding address. Then add units (flats/floors) under Units. Once published, your building gets a QR / Public page — print it for the lobby.

How do I generate rent invoices and collect payments?

Invoices → ＋ Generate invoice selects the month, lease and computes TDS automatically. Tenants pay via bKash/Nagad/bank — record the payment in Payments and a digitally-signed receipt is created instantly. You can email the invoice or receipt with one click.

What does the AI caretaker (KR) do?

KR reads your portfolio and answers questions in plain language — occupancy, rent roll, unpaid invoices, upcoming renewals, tax deadlines. Open the 🤖 AI Caretaker module in the dashboard or use the chat widget.

Can my tenants log in?

Yes. Tenants get their own portal (via the Portal action on their row) — they can see their lease, pay rent online, view receipts and raise maintenance tickets without seeing any other data.

How do I give my manager or accountant access?

Superadmin/Owner → Team → invite by email. Each member gets a role (manager, accountant, service manager, legal…) and sees only the modules that role needs.
