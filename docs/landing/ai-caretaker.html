Meet KR — your AI caretaker

Not a chatbot bolted on. A responsible agent that acts inside your permissions — invoices, tickets, compliance checks and legal answers.

What KR can do for you

🧾 Generate invoices

"Generate June invoice for L-003" — KR creates it with the correct TDS split.

🔧 Create maintenance tickets

"Report a roof leak at Flat 3B" — liability resolved, ticket routed, partner suggested.

⚖️ Legal answers

"What's the legal advance limit?" — answered from PRCA 1991 via retrieval, not guesswork.

🏦 Tax calculations

"Holding tax for Banani Tower?" — the full Net Annual Value pipeline, step by step.

💬 Tenant Q&A

Tenants ask about their lease and rights — scoped to their own unit only.

📅 Reminders

"Remind me about the TDS PSR deadline" — calendar-aware, proactive.

How it works

  1. Understand — natural language over your portfolio and Bangladesh's property law (RAG over statute texts).
  2. Act — tool calls execute real operations: invoices, tickets, calculations.
  3. Stay in bounds — every action inherits your role permissions. A tenant's KR cannot touch lease terms; an owner's KR cannot see other portfolios.
  4. Explain — KR shows the tools it used and the reasoning, so nothing happens invisibly.
Guardrails: KR never performs evictions or lockouts — it generates compliant notices for legal review. It never exposes one tenant's data to another. It flags, rather than hides, compliance risks.
