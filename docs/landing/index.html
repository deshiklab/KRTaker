Law, encoded

We turned Bangladesh's property law into guardrails so you never fall out of compliance.

TRANSFER OF PROPERTY ACT 1882 · §107

Registration gate

Leases over 12 months can't activate ledgers until the sub-registry deed is verified.

PRCA 1991 · §10 / §23

Advance cap

Residential advances are capped at 1 month's rent without rent-controller consent.

PRCA 1991 · §18

Eviction protection

No digital lockouts — only compliant notices when grounds (3+ months unpaid) are met.

PRCA 1991 · §13

Signed receipts

Every payment issues a cryptographically signed rent receipt for both parties.

INCOME TAX ACT 2023

TDS automation

Withholding split, PSR tracking and +50% penalty logic for missing documentation.

E-PORCHA / LAND.GOV.BD

Khatian verification

CS/SA/RS/BS khatian cross-reference before onboarding any asset.
