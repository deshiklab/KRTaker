Frequently asked questions

Everything owners, tenants, partners and NRBs ask us.

KRTaker (Key Responsibility Taker) is a SaaS property-management platform for Bangladesh with an AI caretaker at its core. It handles tenants, leases, rent collection, taxes, maintenance and legal compliance for property owners — while tenants, partners and legal firms each get their own scoped workspace.
Property owners (subscribers), service partners (B2B contractors), legal firms/consultants, and tenants (invited by their landlord). Platform staff accounts (Service Manager, CRM, Accountant, HR) are provisioned by the KRTaker operations team.
