About KRTaker

We're builders who got tired of watching landlords drown in spreadsheets, agents and legal ambiguity.

Why we exist

Dhaka and Chattogram's urban boom created a generation of property owners — and a mountain of manual responsibility. Tenants, leases, taxes, repairs, registration deadlines, disputes. For local investors the cost is time; for NRBs it's distance. KRTaker (Key Responsibility Taker) exists to take that responsibility on — with software that understands Bangladesh's law and payments, and an AI caretaker that never sleeps.

Our principles

⚖️ Law-first

Compliance isn't a checkbox — it's the foundation. We encode statutes so users are protected by default.

🔒 Data-isolated

Multi-tenant by architecture. Your portfolio is yours alone — row-level isolation, role-scoped views.

🤖 Human-in-the-loop AI

KR acts, but never silently — every action is transparent, reversible and permission-bound.

🇧🇩 Built for Bangladesh

bKash, Nagad, city corporations, khatians, NBR — local reality first, international polish second.
