Bringing rental income home

Bangladesh Bank allows rental yield and property sale proceeds to be credited to Non-Resident Taka Accounts (NRTA) and Non-Resident Investor's Taka Accounts (NITA), subject to documentation. KRTaker generates the dossier:

  1. Registered lease agreements (from your compliance engine)
  2. Tax clearance certificate (TCC) reminders
  3. Form IT10B — return of assets & liabilities
  4. Signed rent receipts (PRCA §13)
  5. Form TM (outward remittance) & Form QA-22 where applicable
