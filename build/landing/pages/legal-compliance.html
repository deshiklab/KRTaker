Bangladesh property law is unforgiving to the uninformed. We encoded it.

Why compliance matters

Unregistered leases are inadmissible in court (Transfer of Property Act 1882 §49). Overcharged advances are recoverable. Unlawful evictions carry penalties. Most landlords discover these rules after a dispute — KRTaker prevents them before one starts.

The statutes we enforce

Transfer of Property Act, 1882

§105 lease definition · §107 registered instrument for >12-month terms · §49 inadmissibility.

Registration Act, 1908

§17(1)(d) mandatory registration of long leases — our gate lives here.

Premises Rent Control Act, 1991

§10/§23 advance cap · §13 receipts · §15/§16 rent revision · §18 eviction protection.

Income Tax Act, 2023

§109/§128 TDS on rent · §115 developer payments · §125 transfers · surcharge brackets.

Withholding Tax Rules, 2024

Rates, PSR obligations and the +50% penalty for missing documentation.

Land Records (e-Porcha)

Khatian cross-reference (CS/SA/RS/BS) via land.gov.bd & eporcha.gov.bd.
