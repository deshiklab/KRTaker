Your rights (PRCA 1991, simplified)

Advance cap

Residential security deposit cannot exceed one month's rent without a rent-controller consent order (§10/§23).

Receipts

You're entitled to a signed receipt for every payment (§13) — KRTaker issues them automatically.

Eviction protection

While paying allowable rent you cannot be summarily evicted; eviction requires a court decree (§18).

Maintenance

Structural repairs are the landlord's responsibility; day-to-day care is yours. Neglect → deduction rights.
